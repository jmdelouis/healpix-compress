\subsection{Where on the Sphere the Advantage Lives}
\label{sec:latitude}
\input{tables/table_latitude_effect}

HEALPix cells are equal in area but not in shape. Their local elongation is
smallest in the equatorial belt ($|\mathrm{lat}|<41.81^\circ$, where the
pixelization is a simple cylindrical arrangement) and grows through the
polar caps. That equal-area pixelizations obtain their area guarantee at the
cost of a latitude-dependent shape distortion is a general property of
discrete global grids~\cite{kmoch2022distortions}; the narrower question
here is whether this distortion reaches the storage result.
Table~\ref{tab:latitude_effect} correlates the \emph{intrinsic}
($\gamma_{\mathrm{tile}}=1$) ratio of each region with $|\mathrm{latitude}|$
and compares the two regimes directly.

These tests form a second family and carry their own Holm correction across
the eight reported targets. One association survives it: at the tightest
storage budget the equatorial belt reaches a median intrinsic ratio of
$0.95$ against $1.21$ in the caps ($\rho=0.50$,
$p^{\mathrm{Holm}}_\rho=0.009$; belt-versus-cap $p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.008$).
Two further contrasts fall just outside the threshold and are reported as
such rather than as findings: the loosest RMSE target, $0.97$ against $1.20$
($p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.077$), and the $5\%$ budget
($p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.077$). Nothing at the three tightest RMSE targets
approaches significance. This is not read as evidence of no influence, but
as an indication that any influence is smaller than this sample can resolve
where the intrinsic gap is large.

Taken at face value, the surviving association indicates that the regime in
which HEALPix becomes competitive at a given overhead is reached in the belt
before the caps, and the two near-threshold contrasts point in the same
direction. The result is presented as exploratory. Latitude is confounded
with scene class, spatial spectrum, orientation relative to UTM and cell
anisotropy, and none of the last three has been measured, so a correlation
with a coordinate is not a correlation with the geometric quantity suspected
of causing it. The measurement that would settle the question, the local
Jacobian of the UTM-to-HEALPix map with its singular-value ratio and
orientation per tile, entered as covariates alongside scene class and a
spectral-slope proxy, is available in principle. Until it is made, this
subsection is kept out of the abstract.
