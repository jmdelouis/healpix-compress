\subsection{Where on the Sphere the Advantage Lives}
\label{sec:latitude}
HEALPix cells are equal in area and differ in shape: their local
elongation is smallest in the equatorial belt ($|\mathrm{lat}|<41.81^\circ$)
and grows through the polar caps~\cite{kmoch2022distortions}. Table~\ref{tab:latitude_effect}
asks whether that distortion reaches the storage result. It correlates
the overhead-free ($\gamma_{\mathrm{tile}}=1$) ratio of each region with
$|\mathrm{latitude}|$ (Spearman's $\rho$). Its last four columns
compare the two regimes with a two-sided Mann--Whitney test.

These tests form two families of eight (the rank correlation and the
belt-versus-cap comparison), each Holm-corrected within itself. Two associations survive it, both at a matched
storage budget. At the tightest budget the equatorial belt reaches a
median overhead-free ratio of $0.95$ against $1.21$ in the caps
($\rho=0.50$, $p^{\mathrm{Holm}}_\rho=0.009$; belt-versus-cap
$p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.008$), and at the $5\%$ budget $2.67$ against $2.81$
(belt-versus-cap $p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.039$, the rank correlation itself
outside the threshold at $0.128$). The loosest \term{RMSE target} points the
same way without reaching it, $0.90$ against $1.02$
($p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.188$), and nothing at the three tightest RMSE
\term{targets} approaches significance: where the gap is large, the influence
is smaller than this sample can resolve. Taken at face value, the
surviving association says that the regime in which HEALPix becomes
competitive at a given overhead is reached in the belt before the caps.
The result is exploratory: latitude is confounded with scene class,
spatial spectrum, orientation relative to UTM and cell anisotropy. One of
these is measured. The spectral slope $\beta$ of each crop
(Section~\ref{sec:support_controls}) is unrelated to latitude in this
sample ($\rho=0.05$, $p=0.76$). The association at the tightest budget
survives holding it fixed (partial Spearman $\rho=0.52$, $p=0.001$,
$n=40$), so scene texture as the slope measures it leaves the latitude
effect intact. Orientation relative to UTM and cell anisotropy
remain unmeasured; the local Jacobian of the UTM-to-HEALPix map per
tile, entered as a covariate with scene class, would settle it. This
subsection therefore stays out of the abstract.
