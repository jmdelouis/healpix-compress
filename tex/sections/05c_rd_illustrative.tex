\subsection{One Illustrative Operating Point: a 30\% Granule Overhead}
\label{sec:rd_illustrative}

The last block of Table~\ref{tab:synthesis} and Table~\ref{tab:error_at_size}
evaluate the comparison at $\gamma_{\mathrm{tile}}=1.30$, the
illustrative \term{overhead} scenario of Section~\ref{sec:overhead}. Every
verdict in them is the curve of Section~\ref{sec:rd_main} read at one
abscissa: HEALPix is the smaller representation exactly where $1.30$
exceeds that \term{target}'s $\gamma_\star$, which is why the sign changes
between $10^{-3}$ and $3\times10^{-3}$ and nowhere else. In sizes: if the
native product is stored with an illustrative $30\%$ layout \term{overhead}, the
Clough--Tocher \term{carrier} is $1.59$ times its size at the tightest tolerance
and $0.72$ times at the loosest. Each entry is a per-region HPX/UTM ratio
with the oracle-best codec chosen per region and per grid, the UTM side
carrying $\gamma_{\mathrm{tile}}=1.3$, so a value below one favours
HEALPix. The median is over the $n$ evaluable regions.
$p^{\mathrm{Holm}}$ is a two-sided Wilcoxon test on the log ratio,
corrected over the family of eight \term{targets} the two tables report
together. A \term{target} the corrected test does not separate at the $5\%$
level is marked inconclusive whatever the side of its median.

At \term{matched RMSE} (Table~\ref{tab:synthesis}), native UTM is ahead at
the three tightest \term{targets}: HEALPix needs $1.23$--$1.59\times$ the size of
the native product under the illustrative \term{overhead} from $10^{-3}$ to
$10^{-4}$, with Holm-adjusted $p\le0.016$. The order reverses at
$10^{-2}$, where HEALPix needs $0.72\times$ that size over the 32 regions
whose contrast admits the \term{target} (Holm-adjusted $p<10^{-4}$). Between
them, $3\times10^{-3}$ lies near parity (median $0.92$, $n=37$) and is the
only reported \term{target} that fails correction ($p^{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.14$); it
is inconclusive, since no equivalence margin has been defined. Keeping
the excluded regions as bounds would change no verdict
(Appendix~\ref{app:censoring}). In
Table~\ref{tab:error_at_size} the budget is a fraction of the shared raw
HEALPix \term{tile} size, \SI{4194304}{\byte}, the UTM side receives that budget
divided by $\gamma_{\mathrm{tile}}$, and the ratio compared is of
reconstruction errors.

At \term{matched budget} the same regime-dependent pattern holds, and every
\term{target} survives correction. At the $10\%$ and $5\%$ budgets the
reconstruction RMSE of HEALPix is $4.01\times$ and $1.33\times$ that of
the native product under the illustrative \term{overhead}. At the tightest
budget ($2\%$) the order reverses, and HEALPix reaches $0.83\times$ the
UTM RMSE over all 40 regions (Holm-adjusted $p<10^{-4}$).

The region-to-region spread behind each median follows the scene class.
Urban regions are the most favourable to UTM at tight \term{targets} (median
$1.67$ at $10^{-4}$) and, with agriculture, the most favourable to
HEALPix at the loosest one ($0.70$ and $0.69$ at $10^{-2}$). Water
regions produce nearly all the extreme ratios in either direction. Over
near-constant water the compressed files fall below \SI{10}{kB}, where
container \term{overhead} and resampling artefacts dominate; this is why
medians are reported. Figure~\ref{fig:rd_comparison} shows
the absolute-value curves, medians over scenes at each \term{target}.
