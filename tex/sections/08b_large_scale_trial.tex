\section{A Trial of a Conversion Chain on Whole Sentinel-2 Products}
\label{app:large_scale_trial}
The study above compresses one band of one tile per region. This
appendix reports one trial, in October 2026, of a HEALPix conversion
chain under development, run end to end on ten whole Sentinel-2 L2A
products, every band and group. It is a snapshot: a future production
may change any of the settings below.

\paragraph{Chain}
The products are EOPF Zarr products of the 2026 sample service, chosen
for varied landscapes (Table~\ref{tab:operational_sizes}), with cloud
cover below $4\%$ except the volcanic and glacier tile ($78\%$). They
were converted with the legacy-converters L2A preset and healpix-convert
as installed on the GRID4EARTH JupyterHub in October 2026; the
reflectance used healpix-resample at commit \texttt{4786405}
($\ge$ v2026.7.0) and the nearest-neighbour groups v2026.6.0. The
reflectance at \SI{10}{m}, \SI{20}{m} and \SI{60}{m} goes to levels 20,
19 and 17 by the PSF-aware \term{transfer}~\cite{delouis2026resample}
(9 nearest pixels per cell, weight threshold $0.1$, damping
$\lambda=0.1$, at most 100 conjugate-gradient iterations; the 40-region
sweeps of the body use $\lambda=10^{-3}$, so their PSF-aware figures do
not carry over unchanged), one level-11 cell per chunk. Masks, scene
classification, atmosphere, probabilities and footprints are transferred
by nearest neighbour and stored losslessly.

\paragraph{Codecs}
Both codecs of this repository (Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}) work
on the digital numbers (DN) of the product, 1~DN being $10^{-4}$ in
reflectance, on the chunk de-interleaved into its raster in face
indices. \texttt{hcc-sentinel2} stores that raster under lossless
JPEG~XL. \texttt{hcc-sentinel2-pyramid} stores the reflectance in two
layers: the parent level, the mean of the valid children rounded to DN
and coded losslessly; and the detail, the children minus a least-squares
prediction from the $5\times5$ neighbourhood of parents, quantised with
a uniform step $s=\max(1~\mathrm{DN}, r\,\sigma)$, where $\sigma$ is the
standard deviation of the chunk's detail and $r=0.57$. The error of
every cell is at most $s/2$, and the step follows the detail of each
chunk, as an error budget proportional to the local \term{transfer}
error asks.

\paragraph{Size}
Over the ten products (Table~\ref{tab:operational_sizes}), the HEALPix
reflectance is $1.50$ times the lossless JPEG~2000 reflectance of the
UTM tile ($1.48$ in median, $1.36$ to $1.90$ by tile) and $1.16$ times
the EOPF reflectance; the whole product grows from 10.7 to 12.4~GB, by
$16\%$. These are not the \term{matched-error} ratios of
Section~\ref{sec:results}: the HEALPix reflectance is lossy, while the
JPEG~2000 tile and the EOPF product are lossless on the digital numbers.
They say what the HEALPix copy costs next to the product as distributed;
a UTM product compressed to the same error would be smaller than its
lossless form, and the ratio larger. The \term{matched-error} ratio of
this chain is not measured, and the trial did not write the derived
levels of a multiresolution pyramid. The ratio is highest where the UTM
tile is cheapest, the arid tile ($1.90$) and the coastal tile with open
sea ($1.70$).

\paragraph{Quality}
The error is measured on the native UTM pixels of 12 random interior
level-11 chunks per tile and resolution, read back with the observation
operator of the \term{transfer}, each band's no-data pixels excluded.
The \term{transfer} alone leaves $5$ to $6$~DN RMS at \SI{10}{m}, under
$1\%$ of the scene's standard deviation (the 40-region sweep, read the
same way at $\lambda=10^{-3}$, finds a floor several times larger,
Table~\ref{tab:common_two_readers}; the two are not reconciled here).
The codec adds $1.56$ times that error in median at \SI{10}{m} and
\SI{20}{m}, the ratio its default was designed for, within $1.39$ to
$1.63$ per tile and band at \SI{10}{m} and $1.42$ to $1.65$ at
\SI{20}{m}, except B01, whose \term{transfer} error is below 1~DN. At
\SI{60}{m} the ratio falls to $0.65$, because the \term{transfer} error
at level 17 is three to eight times its value at \SI{20}{m}. The radial
power spectrum of the residual, \term{transfer} and compression together,
is white, with no peak at the chunk size or any other scale, and its RMS
lies $19$ to $141$ times below the standard deviation of $256\times256$
patches by tile and band. The per-band table, the spectra and the
notebook that draws them are in the repository
(\texttt{test/validation\_s2\_ten\_tiles.ipynb}).

\paragraph{Scope}
This trial lies outside the deposited chain. Its scripts
(\texttt{test/s2\_full\_products.py}, \texttt{test/s2\_ten\_tiles\_tables.py})
are in the repository, its inputs are not in the data deposit, and the
checker of Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility} does not assert its
figures. It is reported as the outcome of one run.
