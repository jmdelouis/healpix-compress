\subsection{Which Codec to Use on a HEALPix Chunk}
\label{sec:codec_choice}
\input{tables/table_codec_ranking}

Table~\ref{tab:codec_ranking} aggregates every matched-quality and matched-size operating point on the HEALPix side and ranks the codecs by how often each is the best choice. The 400 operating points comprise the 40 regions at all ten swept targets, including the two storage budgets withheld from Tables~\ref{tab:storage_at_rmse}--\ref{tab:error_at_size} for insufficient bracketing, since a per-point ranking does not require the target to be bracketed on both grids. JPEG~2000 is best at 166 points and JPEG~XL at 149, against 37 for SZ3, 35 for ZFP and 13 for the DWT+Zstandard baseline; the mean ranks are $1.85$, $2.25$, $2.71$, $4.48$ and $3.60$. Every codec is evaluable at almost every point, so the ordering is not an artefact of sweep coverage. The robust conclusion is that JPEG~2000 and JPEG~XL form the leading pair, rather than a strict ordering between the two: the counts are descriptive, the points are not independent (the same regions recur across targets), and no BD-rate summary, which would order the pair properly, has been computed.
