\subsection{Which Codec to Use on a HEALPix Chunk}
\label{sec:codec_choice}

An operating point is one (region, \term{target}) pair over the 40 regions and
the five RMSE plus five budget \term{targets}, four hundred in all. A point is
kept when at least one codec is evaluable there. Each point has exactly
one best codec, and \emph{evaluable} counts the points where a codec
produced a value at all.

\input{tables/table_codec_ranking}

Table~\ref{tab:codec_ranking} ranks the codecs on the HEALPix side by
how often each is best. The 400 points include the two storage budgets
withheld from Tables~\ref{tab:synthesis} and~\ref{tab:error_at_size},
since a per-point ranking does not need the \term{target} bracketed on both
grids. JPEG~2000 is best at 199 points and JPEG~XL
at 131, against 35 for ZFP, 26 for SZ3 and 9 for the DWT+Zstandard
baseline; the mean ranks are $1.62$, $2.33$, $4.54$, $2.79$ and $3.68$.
Every codec is evaluable at almost every point, so sweep coverage does not
drive the order. JPEG~2000 and JPEG~XL form the leading pair. A strict
order between the two is not claimed: the counts are descriptive, the
points are not independent (the same regions recur across \term{targets}), and
no BD-rate summary has been computed. The same pair
leads on the native UTM side, and Section~\ref{sec:same_codec} reports
what each codec says about the grid comparison when held fixed on both
sides. The byte-level audit of every encoded point, and the meaning of
``lossless'' after the 16-bit requantisation the image codecs require,
are in Appendix~\ref{app:codec_audit}.
