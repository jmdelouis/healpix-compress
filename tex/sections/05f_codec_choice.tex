\subsection{Which Codec to Use on a HEALPix Chunk}
\label{sec:codec_choice}

An operating point is one (region, target) pair over the 40 regions and the
five RMSE plus five budget targets, four hundred in all, kept when at least
one codec is evaluable there. Wins sum to the number of points, since each
point has exactly one best codec; \emph{evaluable} counts the points where a
codec produced a value at all.

\input{tables/table_codec_ranking}

Table~\ref{tab:codec_ranking} aggregates every matched-quality and
matched-size operating point on the HEALPix side and ranks the codecs by
how often each is best. The 400 points include the two storage budgets
withheld from Tables~\ref{tab:synthesis} and~\ref{tab:error_at_size}
for insufficient bracketing, since a per-point ranking does not need the
target bracketed on both grids. JPEG~2000 is best at 196 points and JPEG~XL
at 131, against 35 for ZFP, 28 for SZ3 and 10 for the DWT+Zstandard
baseline; the mean ranks are $1.63$, $2.35$, $4.55$, $2.78$ and $3.66$.
Every codec is evaluable at almost every point, so sweep coverage does not
drive the order. JPEG~2000 and JPEG~XL form the leading pair; a strict
order between the two is not claimed, because the counts are
descriptive, the points are not independent (the same regions recur
across targets), and no BD-rate summary has been computed. The same pair
leads on the native UTM side, and Section~\ref{sec:same_codec} reports
what each codec says about the grid comparison when held fixed on both
sides. The byte-level audit of every encoded point, and the meaning of
``lossless'' after the 16-bit requantisation the image codecs require,
are in Appendix~\ref{app:codec_audit}.
