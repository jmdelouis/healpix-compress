Every operating point was written to a file, closed, re-opened and decoded by a reader independent of the encoder; 424 of 424 points reproduced the encoder's array exactly. \emph{Self-contained} asks whether the file alone suffices to decode; \emph{sidecar} is the number of bytes a decoder needs that the reported size does not charge (the float32 minimum and span of the 16-bit renormalisation). The 16-bit floor is the RMSE that renormalisation costs before any compression, which is why points through it are reported as lossless \emph{after requantisation} rather than lossless.
