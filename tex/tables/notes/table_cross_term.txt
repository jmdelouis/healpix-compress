Both errors are taken on the level-20 cells and in the same norm; $\rho$ is their normalised inner product, so the exact relation is $D_{\mathrm{total}}/D_0=\sqrt{1+\kappa^2+2\rho\kappa}$ and the quadrature form used in Appendix~\ref{app:error_budget} is its $\rho=0$ case. Operating points are grouped around each reported $\kappa$ within a factor of $1.5$. The last two columns are the growth of the product's total error as measured, and as quadrature predicts it.
