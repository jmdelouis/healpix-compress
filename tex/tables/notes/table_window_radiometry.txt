Each stored tile against its own native window, sampled at the tile's cell centres by nearest pixel, over the 40 regions. Nearest-pixel sampling is cruder than the Clough--Tocher transfer that built the tile, so the two differ even when the window is the right product; that difference is additive noise on the regressor, which attenuates the regression slope by exactly the squared correlation. The ratio slope$/$corr$^2$ is therefore one whatever the noise level and whatever the scene's contrast, and it is the test: a window that is a different acquisition carries a gain and an offset of its own and breaks the identity. The median over all 40 regions is 1.0032. water/lake\_geneva fails all three at once, and by a margin no interpolation choice produces: a rebuild of its stored tile from its own window reproduces neither the level-19 carrier nor the level-20 one. Nothing read from a stored tile is affected; the measurements that re-run a transfer from the window exclude it and say so.
