On 40 regions: the RMSE of the \emph{uncompressed} carrier, read on the native pixels of the footprints' intersection, against the Sentinel-2 observation. \emph{nearest} is the containing cell, the read used throughout the paper; \emph{bilinear} interpolates within the carrier's own lattice at sub-cell position; \emph{psf} is the companion's forward operator, reported only on the tiles it is matched to. Ratios are paired per region, then the median is taken.
