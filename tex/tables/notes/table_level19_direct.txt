Median over the 39 regions of the RMSE of each carrier against the same Sentinel-2 observation, on the intersection mask of Section~\ref{sec:common_reference}, each read at the nearest containing cell of its own level. \emph{Direct} evaluates the Clough--Tocher interpolant of the same native samples at the level-19 cell centres; \emph{aggregate} averages the four level-20 children, which is the exact cell-average of the stored carrier and the object Section~\ref{sec:level_choice} measures. The last column counts the regions on which that carrier has the lower floor. The two level-19 carriers are genuinely different objects, differing by 6.0\% of a scene's standard deviation in RMS, 0.27 times the level-19 floor itself, yet their floors agree to 0.2\% in median and to within $5\%$ on 38 of the 39 regions, so the level-19 penalty is a property of the level rather than of which object is called the carrier. The widest departure is water/lake\_baikal, at 1.051. One region is excluded and reported separately (water/lake\_geneva): this comparison rebuilds the transfer from the native window, and the audit of Appendix~\ref{app:window_radiometry} finds its window is not the product its stored tile was built from, so a rebuild reproduces neither level.
