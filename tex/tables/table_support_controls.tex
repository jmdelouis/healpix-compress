The rotated HEALPix tile and the axis-aligned equal-area crop enclose the same nominal area but not the same ground; these controls bound what that mismatch contributes. The synthetic rows evaluate one analytic random field --- a power law of slope $\beta$ times a Gaussian instrument response of 11.9\,m FWHM, both fitted to the imagery itself --- at both lattices' sample positions. The common-support rows compress the largest common-ground region on each native lattice and normalise by the ground area each covers.
